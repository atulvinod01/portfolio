Markets, 2026

Swing scanner

Every weekday at 16:30 IST it scans the Nifty 200 and publishes up to three paper trades, or none when the market says no.

How it decides

It reads the market first. When the index is below its 50-day average and breadth is weak, it opens nothing and keeps a watchlist. Otherwise three detectors look for pullbacks to the 20-day average, base breakouts on volume and relative-strength leaders at 52-week highs.

How it keeps itself honest

Every published signal is replayed against later prices, so results are tracked in R, not remembered. Stocks with results due inside the holding window are skipped, and a news check can only downgrade a trade, never promote one.

Fraud and risk

Risk engine

A fraud detector that explains itself, so the people who act on an alert can see why it fired.

The problem

Fraud teams drown in alerts. Real signals get lost in operational noise, and analysts stop trusting the scores.

What I built

A hybrid anomaly detector: statistical checks and ML classifiers together, with SHAP turning every score into the few risk drivers behind it, in words a manager can act on.

The result

A 40% reduction in financial risk exposure, faster security audits, and dashboards executives read without a translator.

Portfolios

Portfolio lab

A Monte Carlo engine that finds the best-Sharpe portfolio from live prices. The cloud on the home page is a small version of it.

What it does

Pulls live prices with yfinance, simulates thousands of random weightings, finds the portfolio with the best return per unit of risk, and reports its Value at Risk.

Why it's useful

The efficient frontier is interactive, so you can see what you give up in return for every step down in risk, instead of reading one optimised answer.

Python, NumPy, yfinance, Streamlit
