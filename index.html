Swing scanner for the Nifty 200

Every weekday at 16:30 IST, after the market closes, it scans the Nifty 200 for long-only swing setups and publishes up to three paper trades with entry, stop and target.

It reads the market first. When the index is below its 50-day average and breadth is weak, it opens nothing and keeps a watchlist. Every published signal is replayed against later prices, so the results are tracked in R, not guessed.

Enterprise risk management

The problem

Fraud teams drown in alerts. Real signals get lost in operational noise, and analysts stop trusting the scores.

What I built

A hybrid anomaly detector: statistical checks and ML classifiers together, with SHAP turning every score into the few risk drivers behind it, in words a manager can act on.

The result

A 40% reduction in financial risk exposure, faster security audits, and dashboards executives read without a translator.

Python, scikit-learn, SHAP, Streamlit
